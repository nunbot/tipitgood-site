Tip It Good Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · Mud Hut Software L.L.C.

The short version

Tip It Good does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It does not use the internet, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What the app stores

One setting, on your device only: the tip preset you last used, so it is ready next time. Amounts you type are never saved. Deleting the app deletes the setting.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive the amounts you type, your tips, or anything else from the app.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions? Email support@tipitgood.app.
