Tip It Good Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · Lighthouse Consulting LLC

Tip It Good doesn't collect any information about you. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app remembers the last tip preset you picked, so it's ready next time. That's all. Bill amounts are never saved, and deleting the app removes the setting.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Email support@tipitgood.app.
