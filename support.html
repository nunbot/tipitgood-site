How to use

Set the tip
Drag the slider, or tap 15%, 18%, 20%, or 25%.
Round the total
Tap the down or up arrow next to the total to nudge it to an even amount. The tip percent updates to match.
Split the bill
Tap Split, then slide across the people, tap one, or use − and +. Up to 20 people.
Start over
Shake your phone or tap ✕ next to the bill. Tap Undo if you didn't mean it.
